# How to prepare for mock tests

Attempt every mock test in exam conditions with a timer and no phone. After the test, spend at least as much time analysing mistakes as you spent writing it. Keep an error notebook with three columns: the question, why you got it wrong (concept, calculation or silly mistake), and the correct method. Revise the error notebook every Saturday.

# Daily study plan

Faculty recommend 6 to 7 hours of self-study on non-class days. Use the 1-3-7 revision rule: revise a topic 1 day, 3 days and 7 days after it is taught. Solve at least 30 questions per subject per day. Sleep 7 hours; late-night studying lowers test scores.

# Physics tips

Draw a free-body diagram for every mechanics problem. Memorise dimensional formulas to check answers quickly. Most errors in JEE Physics come from units and sign conventions.

# Chemistry tips

For NEET and JEE, NCERT is the most important book for Inorganic Chemistry. Make flash cards for reaction mechanisms in Organic Chemistry. Physical Chemistry needs daily numerical practice.

# Biology tips

Read NCERT Biology line by line at least three times. Diagrams from NCERT are often asked directly in NEET. Use the academy's chapter-wise question bank after each NCERT reading.

# Doubt clearing

Doubt sessions are held every day from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM at each branch. Students can also post doubts in the app; faculty reply within 24 hours.
