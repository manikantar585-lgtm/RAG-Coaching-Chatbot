# Courses offered

Vidya Coaching Academy (fictional sample institute) runs classroom and online coaching for JEE Main & Advanced, NEET-UG, TS EAMCET, and Banking/SSC exams. All courses include printed study material, weekly tests, and doubt-clearing sessions.

# JEE Main and Advanced (2-year program)

For students entering Intermediate 1st year (Class 11). Covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Classes run 6 days a week. Includes 40 full-length mock tests in the second year.
Fee: Rs 1,20,000 per year. It can be paid in 3 instalments: 50% at admission, 30% in September, 20% in December.

# JEE Crash Course

A 10-week revision program starting every January for Class 12 students. Fee: Rs 25,000. Includes 15 mock tests and a formula booklet.

# NEET-UG (2-year program)

For Biology (BiPC) students in Class 11. Covers Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology with NCERT-line-by-line coverage. Includes 35 full-length mock tests.
Fee: Rs 1,10,000 per year, payable in 3 instalments like the JEE program.

# TS EAMCET (1-year program)

For Class 12 MPC or BiPC students in Telangana. Weekend and evening batches available. Fee: Rs 45,000 for the year. Bilingual classes in English and Telugu.

# Banking and SSC Foundation

A 6-month course for graduates covering Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English and General Awareness. Fee: Rs 18,000. Online batch fee: Rs 12,000.

# Scholarships

The Vidya Scholarship Test (VST) is held on the second Sunday of every month. Scores above 90% get a 50% fee waiver, 80-90% get 30%, and 70-80% get 15%. Children of government school teachers and farmers get an additional 10% concession on submission of proof. Scholarships cannot be combined with sibling discounts.

# Sibling discount

If a brother or sister is already enrolled, the second child gets 10% off the tuition fee.
