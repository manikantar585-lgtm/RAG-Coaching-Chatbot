# Batch timings

Morning batch: 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM (for students attending regular college during the day).
Day batch: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM with a lunch break from 1:00 to 1:45 PM (integrated program).
Evening batch: 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
Weekend batch (EAMCET and Banking only): Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

# Branches

Ameerpet branch: near the Metro station, open Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 8 PM.
Dilsukhnagar branch: opposite the bus depot, open Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 8 PM.
Online classes run on our mobile app with live and recorded lectures.

# Attendance policy

Students must maintain at least 80% attendance every month. Parents receive an SMS if the student is absent for 2 consecutive days. Students below 75% attendance for two months may be shifted to a different batch.

# Refund policy

A full refund is given if the student withdraws within 7 days of admission. Between 8 and 30 days, 50% of the paid tuition fee is refunded. No refund is given after 30 days. The admission fee of Rs 2,000 and study material charges are non-refundable. Refund requests must be submitted in writing at the branch office and are processed within 15 working days.

# Exams and results

Weekly tests are held every Sunday. Results and rank lists are published on the app by Tuesday evening. Parent-teacher meetings happen on the last Saturday of every month.

# Hostel

The academy has tie-ups with separate boys' and girls' hostels within 1 km of each branch. Hostel fee is about Rs 9,000 per month including food, paid directly to the hostel.
